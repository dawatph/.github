## About Dawat

**Dawat** is a community-first superapp designed for rural and underserved communities.

It brings transportation, delivery, commerce, work, property, tourism, agriculture, payments, rewards, livestreaming, and community initiatives together inside one platform: **Dawat.ph**.

> Built locally. Powered by community. Designed for everyday life.

## Core Services

**Ride**  
Community transportation for passengers, riders, and drivers.

**Food**  
Ordering and delivery from restaurants, cafes, market stalls, and home-based sellers.

**Delivery**  
Local delivery for parcels, documents, groceries, medicine, and other permitted goods.

**Market**  
Digital storefronts for merchants, makers, farmers, and local producers.

**DawatPay**  
Payments, balances, transfers, rewards, and supported digital transactions inside Dawat.

**Jobs**  
Local work opportunities, service listings, and talent discovery.

**Repair**  
Find skilled workers and repair services for homes, devices, vehicles, and local businesses.

**Property**  
Discover property listings, rentals, land, rooms, and local spaces.

**Tourism**  
Explore accommodations, destinations, guides, cultural experiences, and local attractions.

**Agritrade**  
Connect farmers, buyers, suppliers, transport providers, and agricultural markets.

**Rewards**  
Earn and use points, incentives, and community rewards across Dawat.

**Bayanihan**  
Create and support community campaigns, local needs, food rescue, medical charity, small businesses, and funded projects.

## More From Dawat

**Palibre**  
Community giveaways, sponsored items, free offers, and shared support.

**Directory**  
Discover community-supported stores, businesses, software projects, organizations, and local initiatives.

**Live**  
Livestreaming for creators, merchants, community events, product showcases, and local updates.

**Hackathon**  
Builder challenges, community competitions, and programs for developers and creators.

**Item Market**

**All Services**  
One place to browse the complete Dawat service ecosystem.

## Why Dawat

Many digital platforms are designed around large urban markets. Dawat is being built around the realities of provincial communities, including mountain geography, dispersed barangays, small businesses, agriculture, tourism, local trust, and limited access to logistics.

Our mission is to create useful technology that:

- Opens livelihood opportunities for riders, workers, and service providers
- Helps farmers, merchants, and small businesses reach more customers
- Improves access to transportation, delivery, jobs, property, and local services
- Supports Filipino developers, creators, founders, and community builders
- Strengthens communities without leaving local identity behind

## Contributing

Open an issue before proposing a major change. Keep pull requests focused, clearly documented, and respectful of the communities Dawat is designed to serve.

Never commit passwords, private keys, seed phrases, API secrets, or personal user information.

## Security

Do not disclose security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues.

Send responsible security reports privately to [dev@dawat.ph](mailto:dev@dawat.ph).
